# Pilot Evaluation Protocol

## Goal
Compare adaptive agent-driven VR safety training with a fixed scripted condition.

## Suggested participants
Small convenience sample of volunteer university students for a prototype pilot. Obtain institutional/ethics approval if required before collecting research data.

## Conditions
- Adaptive: backend pedagogical policy changes difficulty, hazard focus, and instructional cues.
- Fixed: predefined hazard sequence independent of learner performance.

## Suggested procedure
1. Consent and demographic/background questionnaire.
2. 3-minute VR familiarization.
3. Pre-test: identify hazards in a neutral construction scene.
4. Training: 8-12 scenarios in assigned condition.
5. Immediate post-test using unseen scene layouts.
6. Engagement/usability questionnaire.
7. Delayed retention test if study logistics allow.

## Primary measures
- Hazard-identification accuracy.
- Correct response/action rate.
- Response time.
- Pre-to-post improvement.
- Retention score.
- Engagement/usability self-report.

## Useful analysis
- Compare normalized learning gain between adaptive and fixed groups.
- Report effect sizes and confidence intervals; treat inferential statistics cautiously for a small pilot.
- Analyze per-hazard mastery to determine whether adaptation improves weak areas.
